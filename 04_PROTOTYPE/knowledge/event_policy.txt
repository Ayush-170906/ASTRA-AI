Technical Event Communication Policy

External announcements policy:
Any announcement, email, or message sent to all registered participants of a technical event
is classified as an external communication action. External communications require explicit
review and approval from the seminar coordinator or event owner before being sent, because they
reach third parties outside the immediate control of the organizing student or system.

This applies regardless of whether the message is sent manually or by an automated system or
agent. Automated systems must never send participant-facing communications without a human
approval step immediately before the send action.

Approval record-keeping:
Every approval or denial of an external communication must be logged with a timestamp and a
reason, whether approved or denied, for audit purposes.

Data handling policy:
No real participant personal data (names, emails, phone numbers) should be used in a prototype
or demonstration system. Demonstrations must use mock or placeholder recipient data only.
