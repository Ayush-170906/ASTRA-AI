Technical Event Planning Guidelines

All technical seminar and workshop events organized by the Information Technology department
must follow the standard checklist below before the event date.

Checklist items:
1. Venue booking confirmed with the college administration.
2. Registration form circulated at least 7 days before the event.
3. Technical setup (projector, microphone, laptop, internet) tested one day in advance.
4. Speaker or presenter coordination confirmed, including backup slides.
5. Feedback form prepared for distribution after the event.

Registration capacity planning:
Event capacity should always be planned with a 10% buffer below the venue's maximum seating
to allow for late walk-ins and volunteer staff. If the venue's maximum seating is 500, the
recommended planned registration capacity is 450, but the hard maximum standard registration
capacity for a single technical event is 500 seats unless a larger venue is booked.

Timeline recommendation:
Announcements should go out 10 days before the event, with a reminder 2 days before the event.
